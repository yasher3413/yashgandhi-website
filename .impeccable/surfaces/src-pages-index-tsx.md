# Surface: home (/) + /book

Scope: the personal site's home route and the booking route. Mode: Experience. The same page also has to Persuade recruiters within seconds.
Audience: recruiters (resume, experience, contact), plus Twitter visitors who should want to explore and share it.
Constraints: keep all content, the live Spotify and Strava data, the Cal.com booking flow, and the Supascribe embed. No theme toggle.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a nine-hole yardage book. Each section is a hole: a tee sign, a surveyed hole diagram, and caddie notes pinned by leader lines. This rejects the dark bento-card dev portfolio and the cream editorial serif page.

OWN-WORLD: The ground is drenched rough green (#14482f) with faint topographic contours that brighten near the cursor. Fairway is striped mid-green, the putting green is bright, bunkers are sand, water is blue, and linework is white. Flag red (#ef3b2c) marks only what is live or current. The scorecard is cool white card stock with green grid ink and red pencil circles. Tee signs are dark plaques. Type: a condensed sport grotesque for numerals and names, a grotesque for body text, mono only for yardages and measurements, and a pencil hand only for caddie notes.

STORY: A visitor tees off at Hole 1 (name, role, resume flag) and plays through About, Experience, Projects, The Book, Blog, Wellness, Listens, and Clubhouse (contact). Their scorecard fills in with a birdie on each hole they visit. At the end they book a tee time (the /book call page) or email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: YASH GANDHI in condensed display at roughly 10–13vw, left-aligned in the top-left half. "Engineer & Operations Analyst" sits below it with the Resume and Contact buttons. The right 55% is a surveyed Hole 1 diagram (tee at bottom-left, dogleg fairway, bunkers, water, green with a red flag top-right) with leader-line notes: Toronto, Western, T-Mobile. An aim line and yardage readout follow the pointer from the ball, and clicking hits a shot. The scorecard nav is fixed along the bottom.

FORM: Yardage book / club scorecard. Candidate #7 on the ordered list, seed key 88849330.

SIGNATURE: Click-to-hit a shot on the Hole 1 diagram (arced flight, a shadow, and a pencilled carry distance). On every other hole, the ball advances shot by shot along the fairway as the matching item scrolls into view. MOTION GRAMMAR: ball flight arcs and ease-out settles, pencil strokes that draw in, nothing bouncy.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
